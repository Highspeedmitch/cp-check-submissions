# Complete an assigned Afterlight resource inspection

**Audience:** Afterlight Resource Network contractors, employees, and owners

**Applies to:** Commercial, residential, long-term rental, and short-term rental assignments

Use this workflow for every inspection assigned through the Resource Portal. Starting from the assignment card ensures that Afterlight uses the correct property access and checklist. For 1099 contractors, it also preserves the agreed compensation and earning record.

## Before you begin

- Read the assignment dates and special instructions. Contractors should also verify the displayed compensation.
- Confirm that you are at the correct property.
- Make sure your device has enough battery and a reliable connection.
- Allow camera or photo-library access if the checklist requires evidence.
- Contact Afterlight before starting if the property, instructions, or compensation appear incorrect.

## Open the correct checklist

1. Find the property under **My Assignments**.
2. Select **Start Inspection** on its card.
3. Confirm the property name at the top of the checklist.
4. Read any **Special assignment instructions** before entering responses.

Do not open a bookmarked inspection form or try to substitute a different property. Contractor access is authorized through the exact scheduled assignment.

## Complete the inspection

1. Answer every required checklist question.
2. When a response opens a follow-up area, describe the location, what you observed, and any action taken.
3. Attach clear photos when requested. Afterlight supports up to six photos per photo field and up to ten photos across one inspection.
4. Review the completed checklist before submitting.
5. Select **Submit Checklist** once.

While files are being processed, the page may display **Preparing secure photo uploads**, **Uploading photo**, **Photos uploaded**, or **Generating your inspection report**. Keep the page open until the confirmation appears.

## Confirm completion

The inspection has been received when the page displays **Inspection complete**.

1. Select **Return to Dashboard**. For a contractor account, this returns you to the Resource Portal.
2. The completed card leaves **My Assignments** after processing.
3. For 1099 contractors, a new earning appears with **Pending approval** after Afterlight records the completed work. Employees and owners do not receive a per-inspection earning.

You do not prepare or send the customer invoice. Customer billing and your contractor earning are separate workflows managed by Afterlight.

## If something goes wrong

- **A required field is highlighted:** Complete it and submit again.
- **An issue requires details or a photo:** Add the requested evidence before resubmitting.
- **The upload pauses or fails:** Keep the page open, check the connection, and use the retry option displayed by the app.
- **The property does not match the assignment:** Stop and return to the portal. Contact Afterlight before submitting anything.
- **The assignment disappears before you finish:** Do not start a new form from a bookmark. Ask the assigning manager whether the work was changed or canceled.
- **You submitted incorrect information:** Contact Afterlight. A completed inspection cannot be edited from the Resource Portal.
