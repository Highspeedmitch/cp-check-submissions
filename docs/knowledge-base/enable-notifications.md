# Enable and troubleshoot notifications

**Audience:** Afterlight organization users and Resource Network users

**Applies to:** In-app workflow alerts and optional browser or device push notifications

Afterlight records important workflow changes inside the application. Push notifications are an optional companion that can alert you when Afterlight is closed. Notification availability can differ by environment, browser, and device.

## Enable notifications

1. Sign in to the Afterlight workspace you normally use.
2. When the notification banner appears, select **Enable Notifications**.
3. Approve the browser or device permission request.
4. Keep Afterlight signed in on that device.

Enable each browser, installed web app, or device separately. Selecting **Not Now** hides the banner on that browser for seven days; it does not disable in-app workflow alerts.

### iPhone and iPad

Apple mobile devices require iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or newer and an installed Home Screen web app:

1. Open Afterlight in Safari.
2. Use **Share**, then select **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Afterlight from the new Home Screen icon and sign in.
4. Select **Enable Notifications** and choose **Allow**.

The permission cannot be enabled from a regular Safari tab on iPhone or iPad.

## Understand the alerts

You receive only events relevant to your role and work. Depending on your access, Afterlight may alert you when:

- an assignment is created, rescheduled, reassigned, canceled, or completed;
- an inspection submission is ready for review;
- an invoice is submitted, approved, returned, queued for AP email, or fails AP delivery;
- an Afterlight service invoice is marked paid;
- a contractor earning is created, approved, or voided;
- a Gusto payout batch is created, submitted, or marked paid; or
- a service-model request needs information or is approved or denied.

Opening the related section marks its in-app alert read. A push opens the relevant Afterlight screen when the operating system and browser provide its route.

An **AP email queued** alert means the email provider accepted the message for processing. It does not prove that the destination mailbox ultimately received it. Review the invoice delivery status in Afterlight if AP reports a problem.

## If something goes wrong

- **The banner says notifications are blocked:** Open the browser or operating-system notification settings for Afterlight, change the permission to Allow, return to Afterlight, and select **Retry**.
- **iPhone or iPad says to add Afterlight to the Home Screen:** Complete the installation steps above, then open the Home Screen version instead of the Safari tab.
- **Notifications are allowed but the device could not be connected:** Confirm that you are online, refresh Afterlight, and select **Retry**. A temporary configuration problem does not remove the in-app workflow record.
- **No banner appears:** Notifications may already be enabled, may have been postponed for seven days, may be unsupported by that browser, or may not be enabled in the current environment.
- **A push does not arrive:** Open Afterlight and review the relevant Dashboard, Billing, Resource Portal, or Service Delivery section. Also check Focus, Do Not Disturb, battery-saving, and browser notification settings.
- **You changed devices or browsers:** Enable notifications again on the new device or browser.

Never rely on push delivery as the only record of an assignment, invoice, or payment status. Afterlight remains the system of record.
