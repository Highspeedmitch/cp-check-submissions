# Use the short-term rental property owner portal

**Audience:** Short-term rental property owners

Use the owner Dashboard to review assigned properties, read property information, open available profit statements, and find the organization contacts responsible for the property.

## Review assigned properties

After signing in, **My Properties** lists only the short-term rental properties assigned to your account. Select a property name or card to make it the current property.

If an expected property is missing, contact an organization administrator or one of the property managers listed in the Dashboard. Property owners cannot assign properties to their own accounts.

## View property information

1. Find the property on the Dashboard.
2. Select **View Info**.
3. Review the access instructions, maintenance information, and general property information supplied by the organization.

Property-owner access is read-only. Contact the property manager when information is missing or incorrect. Do not copy access details into email, text messages, or another system unless the organization has approved that method.

## Open a profit statement

1. Find the property on the Dashboard.
2. Select **Profit Statement**.
3. Review the current-month and year-to-date values, the upload date, and the attached PDF when one is available.

The statement reflects the latest information uploaded by the organization. If Afterlight says that nothing is available yet, return to the Dashboard and contact the property manager. Do not treat an absent statement as a zero balance.

## Contact the property team

The Dashboard lists the organization contacts available to the owner account. Use an organization-approved contact method when requesting a consultation or asking about property information. A consultation date is not confirmed until the property team responds directly.

## If something goes wrong

- **No properties are assigned:** Ask the organization administrator to verify the property-owner assignment for your email address.
- **View Info is blank:** Contact the property manager; the organization controls those property details.
- **The profit statement is missing or old:** Confirm the upload date and ask the property manager when the next statement will be available.
- **A PDF does not open:** Allow pop-ups for Afterlight or open the statement link in a new browser tab.
- **The wrong organization appears:** Sign out and confirm that you are using the invitation and account intended for that property portfolio.
