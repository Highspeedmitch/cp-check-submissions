# Understand your contractor earnings

**Audience:** Afterlight Resource Network contractors

**Applies to:** Assignment compensation, earning approval, and Gusto payment status

Afterlight keeps contractor earnings separate from customer invoices. The Resource Portal shows what Afterlight has recorded for your work; Gusto handles your payment and contractor tax details.

## Before completing an assignment

The assignment card displays **Assignment compensation**. Review that amount before selecting **Start Inspection**.

The amount is snapshotted when the assignment is created. Rescheduling the same assignment does not silently replace the agreed rate with a newer default rate.

If the amount looks incorrect, contact Afterlight before submitting the inspection.

## Read earning statuses

After completed work is processed, the **Earnings** section shows the customer organization, earned date, amount, and status.

| Status | Meaning | What you should do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pending approval** | Afterlight recorded the completed assignment and is reviewing it. | No action unless Afterlight requests information. |
| **Approved** | Afterlight approved the earning for a future payout batch. | No action. |
| **Queued for Gusto** | The earning has been placed into a Gusto payout batch. | Watch Gusto for payment details. |
| **Paid** | Afterlight recorded the Gusto batch as funded. | Confirm receipt through Gusto or your bank. |
| **Void** | Afterlight removed the earning from payment eligibility. | Contact Afterlight if you did not expect this status. |

The **Recorded earnings** summary combines all non-void earning records. It is a history total, not a promise that every displayed dollar is currently payable or has reached your bank.

## What happens in Gusto

- Complete bank-account, direct-deposit, W-9, tax-identification, and tax-form steps only through Gusto.
- Afterlight records Gusto payment progress in the Resource Portal but does not display your sensitive Gusto onboarding information.
- Payment timing and delivery details come from Gusto after an earning is queued.

## Customer invoices are separate

An inspection may also produce a customer-facing invoice, but that invoice represents what the customer owes Afterlight. It does not determine what Afterlight owes you, and you do not need to open Billing or submit an invoice from the Resource Portal.

## Report a discrepancy

Contact Afterlight and provide:

- The property and customer organization.
- The assignment or earned date.
- The amount or status you expected.
- A short description of the discrepancy.

Do not send bank-account numbers, tax identification numbers, passwords, or full Gusto tax forms through Afterlight messages or ordinary email.
