# Use the Weekly War Room

**Audience:** Afterlight platform administrators

**Applies to:** Active Managed Service and Hybrid customer organizations

Use **Platform > Weekly War Room** for an exception-first view of monthly portfolio coverage, work due this week, completion progress, and Hybrid Afterlight commitments.

## Read the summary

The summary at the top combines all active Managed Service and Hybrid portfolios:

- **Active portfolios** includes customer organizations that still have active organization members. Historical organizations with no active access are excluded.
- **Monthly coverage** counts each configured property once when it has scheduled or completed work in the current month.
- **This week remaining** compares scheduled assignments still open with all assignments due Monday through Sunday.
- **At Risk** and **Behind** identify the portfolios to review first.

Search by organization, filter by service model, or select **Needs attention** to focus on At Risk and Behind portfolios.

## Understand portfolio status

Each portfolio receives one status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Complete | Every required property has completed work for the month, no work is missed, and any Hybrid minimum is met. |
| On Track | Every property has completed or scheduled monthly work, no work is missed, and any Hybrid minimum is met. |
| At Risk | Monthly scheduling or Hybrid coverage is incomplete while more than seven days remain in the month. |
| Behind | Work is missed, or a monthly scheduling or Hybrid gap remains with seven or fewer days left in the month. |

A property with multiple assignments is counted once in monthly property coverage. Weekly execution continues to count the individual assignments due during the current Monday-through-Sunday week.

## Read Hybrid coverage

Hybrid cards compare the percentage of the organization's properties assigned to Afterlight with the minimum included in its licensed tier. A property is counted once even when it has multiple Afterlight assignments during the month.

The progress bar shows progress toward the required minimum, and the text below it shows the exact assigned and required property counts.

## Respond to an exception

1. Review the reason shown on the portfolio card.
2. Select **Open Admin View** and enter the support reason when prompted.
3. Open Scheduler in the customer workspace.
4. Schedule uncovered properties, correct missed work, or adjust Hybrid fulfillment as appropriate.
5. Return to the Weekly War Room and select **Refresh** to confirm the updated status.

## Understand notifications

Platform administrators receive:

- One Weekly War Room digest per operational week.
- One Behind alert for an organization when it is identified as Behind during the month.

Alerts appear in the platform notification badge and open the Weekly War Room. Browser or device push delivery also occurs when notifications are enabled. Opening the Weekly War Room marks its current War Room alerts as read.
