# Use the Resource Portal as Afterlight staff or owner

**Audience:** Afterlight employees and owners who perform customer inspections

**Applies to:** Deployed Afterlight staff assignments without contractor payables

Your Resource Portal separates the work you perform for Afterlight customers from any customer organization workspace attached to the same login.

## Understand your relationship

- Platform administrators deploy your resource profile to eligible customer organizations and properties.
- Customer administrators or property managers schedule you with **Afterlight staff** fulfillment.
- You see only explicitly assigned work in the Resource Portal.
- Your completed inspection may create a customer invoice owned by Afterlight, but you cannot see Billing or change that customer amount.
- Employee and owner assignments do not create contractor earnings, Gusto batches, or per-inspection payables.

## Complete assigned work

1. Sign in and select **Resource Portal** if your login also has an organization workspace.
2. Review **My Assignments**, the property, dates, and special instructions.
3. Select **Start Inspection** from the correct assignment card.
4. Complete the checklist and wait for **Inspection complete**.
5. Return to the Resource Portal. The completed assignment leaves the scheduled list after processing.

The inspection report goes to the configured customer recipients. For commercial Afterlight-delivered work, Afterlight also prepares the customer service invoice and sends it to the assigned property manager for review.

## If the wrong workflow appears

- **You see contractor compensation or Earnings:** Ask a platform administrator to confirm that your resource relationship is **Afterlight employee** or **Afterlight owner**, not **1099 contractor**.
- **Billing appears in a customer workspace:** Sign out and back in. Internal employees and Afterlight resources should not receive Billing unless they separately have an active customer-contractor responsibility.
- **An assignment is missing:** Confirm that your profile and deployment are active and that the property is within the deployment scope.
- **The Resource Portal is missing:** Ask a platform administrator to confirm that your existing email is linked to the resource profile, then sign out and back in.
